Sir / Madam,

Under instructions from and on behalf of my client named above, I, the undersigned Advocate, do hereby serve upon you this Statutory Legal Demand Notice under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (as amended up to date), stating as follows:

1. **TRANSACTION & CONSIDERATION:**
   That my client is engaged in [Business / Profession] and you, the Noticee, approached my client on [Date / Period] for [Purpose of transaction / Supply of goods / Rendering of services / Friendly loan]. In discharge of your legally enforceable debt and existing liability towards my client, an amount of **Rs. [Amount in Figures]/- (Rupees [Amount in Words] only)** was due and payable by you to my client against Invoice / Agreement dated [Date/Ref No.].

2. **ISSUANCE OF CHEQUE:**
   That in partial / full discharge of your said legally enforceable debt and liability, you, the Noticee, knowingly and deliberately issued the following cheque drawn on your bank account in favour of my client:
   - **Cheque No.:** [Cheque Number]
   - **Date of Cheque:** [Cheque Date]
   - **Amount:** Rs. [Amount in Figures]/- (Rupees [Amount in Words] only)
   - **Drawee Bank & Branch:** [Drawee Bank Name, Branch Address]
   - **Account No.:** [Account Number, if known]

3. **ASSURANCE BY NOTICEE:**
   That at the time of handing over the aforesaid cheque, you specifically assured and represented to my client that the said cheque was valid and that there would be sufficient funds in your bank account to honour the cheque upon its presentation.

4. **PRESENTATION & DISHONOUR:**
   That acting upon your representations and assurances, my client presented the said cheque for clearance through my client’s banker, namely **[Complainant Bank Name, Branch]** on **[Date of Presentation]**, well within the statutory validity period of three months. However, to the utter shock and dismay of my client, the said cheque was dishonoured and returned unpaid by your banker vide Cheque Return Memo dated **[Date of Bank Return Memo]** with the remarks:
   **"[Dishonour Reason, e.g., 'FUNDS INSUFFICIENT' / 'ACCOUNT CLOSED' / 'PAYMENT STOPPED BY DRAWER']"**.

5. **RECEIPT OF DISHONOUR MEMO:**
   That the said Cheque Return Memo was received by my client on **[Date of Receipt of Memo]**. It is manifest that you issued the said cheque dishonestly, with mala fide intention and with full knowledge that there were insufficient funds in your account, thereby cheating and deceiving my client.

6. **STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & 15-DAY NOTICE:**
   That this statutory legal notice is being issued to you within the prescribed period of **30 (thirty) days** from the date of receipt of the bank return memo by my client, in strict accordance with clause (b) of the proviso to Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

7. **DEMAND & CALL TO ACTION:**
   Now, therefore, I hereby call upon you, the Noticee, to pay to my client the aforesaid cheque amount of **Rs. [Amount in Figures]/- (Rupees [Amount in Words] only)** within a period of **15 (fifteen) days** from the date of receipt of this notice, failing which:
   a) My client shall be constrained to initiate criminal proceedings against you under **Section 138 read with Section 142 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881**, and also under **Sections 406, 420 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**;
   b) Under Section 138, you shall be liable to be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to **two years**, or with fine which may extend to **twice the amount of the cheque**, or with both;
   c) You shall also be held liable to pay all legal costs, interest at the rate of **18% per annum** from the date of dishonour until realization, and expenses incurred by my client in these proceedings.

Please note that a copy of this legal notice is retained in my office for future legal record and action in the competent Court of Law.
