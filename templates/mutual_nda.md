### RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**
A. The Parties are currently engaged in discussions regarding **[Purpose of Agreement, e.g., evaluating a strategic commercial partnership / software vendor engagement / M&A exploration]** (the "Permitted Purpose").
B. In connection with the Permitted Purpose, either Party ("Disclosing Party") may disclose, directly or indirectly, confidential, technical, financial, or proprietary information to the other Party ("Receiving Party").
C. The Parties desire to set forth the terms and conditions governing the protection, use, and disclosure of such Confidential Information.

**NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE MUTUAL COVENANTS CONTAINED HEREIN, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

---

### 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" shall mean all non-public, confidential or proprietary information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or in any tangible form, that is designated as confidential or which, by its nature, ought reasonably to be treated as confidential. This includes without limitation:
- Business plans, financial data, customer lists, vendor details, pricing models.
- Source codes, software architecture, technical algorithms, inventions, patents, designs, trade secrets.
- Any notes, analyses, or documents prepared by the Receiving Party derived from such information.

### 2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information does not include information that:
a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
b) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, without any obligation of confidentiality;
c) is rightfully received from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation;
d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
e) is required to be disclosed pursuant to a valid judicial order or regulatory mandate, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party to enable them to seek a protective order.

### 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY
The Receiving Party agrees:
a) To hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and apply at least a reasonable standard of care (not less than the degree of care applied to its own confidential material).
b) To use Confidential Information solely and exclusively for the Permitted Purpose.
c) Not to disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its directors, employees, legal advisors, and consultants on a strict "need-to-know" basis who have signed confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement.
d) Not to copy, reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any confidential materials without prior written consent.

### 4. TERM AND SURVIVAL
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of **[Agreement Term, e.g., 2 (two) years]** from the Effective Date. The confidentiality and non-use obligations under this Agreement shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a further period of **[Survival Period, e.g., 3 (three) years]**; provided, however, that trade secrets and proprietary source code shall remain confidential perpetually.

### 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Upon written request of the Disclosing Party or upon termination of the Permitted Purpose, the Receiving Party shall, within **15 (fifteen) business days**, return or destroy (and certify destruction in writing) all copies, summaries, and extracts of Confidential Information.

### 6. REMEDIES & INJUNCTIVE RELIEF
The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Consequently, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including temporary and permanent **injunctions and specific performance**, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of India**. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**, before a sole arbitrator appointed mutually by the Parties. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be **[City, e.g., New Delhi / Mumbai / Bengaluru]**, India. Subject to arbitration, the courts at **[City]** shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 8. MISCELLANEOUS
- **Entire Agreement:** This document constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning confidentiality.
- **Severability:** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in full force.
- **Amendments:** Must be in writing signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
